Methodology & Sources

How the flexibility potential is estimated

1
Annual electricity consumptionEstimate electricity consumption for each industrial and commercial segment.
↓
2
Peak demandConvert annual consumption to peak demand using a peak contribution factor representing the share of annual consumption associated with peak periods.
↓
3
Segment peak demandAllocate total C&I peak demand across the industrial and commercial segments.
↓
4
End-use demandDisaggregate segment peak demand across electricity end-uses using their share of energy consumption.
↓
5
Technical potentialEstimate the share of end-use peak demand that can technically be shifted, curtailed or adjusted without significant disruption to productivity.
↓
6
Economic potentialIdentify the portion of technical potential that is potentially cost-effective at the selected Economic Potential Threshold.
£200/MWhDefault Economic Potential Threshold — a moderate incentive amount reflective of historical / projected market revenues.

Geographic allocation

Flexibility is allocated to the 14 DNO licence areas in the underlying screening dataset. GSP ranking is an indicative disaggregation of each DNO result using NESO FES 2025 GSP demand shares; it is not an independently modelled GSP flexibility estimate.

Flexibility characteristics

End-uses are characterised by operational attributes such as notice time, response time and maximum curtailment duration. These characteristics can be used to assess suitability for different flexibility services.

Sources & inputs

  • NESO — GB DNO licence-area GIS boundaries
  • NESO — Future Energy Scenarios (FES) and regional / GSP demand pathway data
  • Electricity consumption data — sectoral consumption inputs used to estimate segment and end-use demand
  • Peak-demand evidence — demand-shape and peak contribution inputs used to translate annual consumption into peak demand
  • Sector and end-use evidence — process, operational and load-characteristic evidence informing technical flexibility assumptions
  • Market evidence — historical and projected flexibility market revenues informing economic hurdle-cost assumptions
Important: The dashboard separates technical potential from economic potential. The default view is technical potential. Economic potential applies the selected threshold to the estimated cost of providing flexibility. These estimates are indicative screening values and should not be interpreted as committed flexibility, a procurement forecast or system value.
